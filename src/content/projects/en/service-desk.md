Service Desk is a web portal in pilot to centralize IT support requests. The team can monitor the queue, filter tickets, and record assignments, conversations, service targets, and resolutions.

The system also brings together ticket history, searchable equipment inventory, team chat, and reports. The pilot keeps access restricted while workflows are being evaluated.
