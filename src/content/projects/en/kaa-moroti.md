The starting point was an experience problem: measuring an ecological footprint often requires prior knowledge and involves tools that are not engaging. This makes it harder for people to understand their impact and sustain better habits.

Ka'a Morotĩ turns that process into a gamified journey. Users complete a quiz, receive an environmental score, join challenges, earn Action Points, and follow their ranking and progress through a dashboard.

I built and deployed the full-stack application with authentication, personalized recommendations, challenges, ranking, and a progress dashboard. Its delivery workflow also automates production and pull-request deployments.
