NEURON is an internal platform for the UFLA Interactive Robotics Study Group. It brings projects, teams, responsibilities, and tasks together in one place.

The overview highlights next steps, tasks, and progress across workstreams. Each project can be tracked through a Kanban workflow with stages, owners, and team information. The system also provides an aggregate dashboard for display on a TV.
